# STRATEGY — Niche A: AI Tools & Workflows

Locked 2026-09-12. This is the operating playbook. Read PROJECT.md first for context and the honest target assessment.

---

## 1. Positioning (the narrow version)

"AI tools" is too broad to rank. The algorithm rewards tight topical matching, so we narrow to:

> **One AI workflow per video that replaces something you currently do by hand.**

Not tool listicles. Not news. Not "top 5 AI tools you NEED." Those are saturated and get no sends.
The unit of content is **a task → the workflow → the result**, shown, not described.

**Why this account wins:** operator has Gemini Pro + Claude Pro + ChatGPT Go and builds software. Most accounts in this niche are affiliate marketers who have never used the tools. Ours can show real output.

**One-line promise for the bio:** *"Real AI workflows. One task, start to finish, every day."*

## 2. The send test (apply to every single video)

Reach in 2026 is driven by **sends per reach** — DM shares. Before producing anything, answer:

> Who does a viewer send this to, and what do they type when they send it?

If the answer isn't obvious and specific ("sends to their group chat with 'bro we can automate the whole assignment'"), the idea is rejected. This is the single hardest filter and the most important one.

## 3. Content pillars

| # | Pillar | Format | Length | Send-driver |
|---|--------|--------|--------|-------------|
| P1 | **Impossible demo** | Screen recording, result shown in first 2s | 15–25s | Disbelief |
| P2 | **Workflow teardown** | Full task start→finish, numbered steps | 35–50s | Utility / save |
| P3 | **One prompt** | Before/after split, prompt on screen | 12–20s | Copy-paste value |
| P4 | **Model vs model** | Same task, 3 models, scored | 30–45s | Argument in comments |
| P5 | **You're using it wrong** | Correction, common mistake | 15–25s | Mild superiority |

Rotate P1→P5 so no two consecutive posts share a format. This is also the defense against the near-duplicate penalty (24h–30d reach suppression).

## 4. The structural rules

**First 2 seconds = the result.** Never the setup. Never "hey guys." Skip rate in the first 3s is what kills distribution. Show the finished thing, then explain how.

**Length discipline:** 12–25s (P1/P3/P5) or 35–50s (P2/P4). Never 26–34s — that's the dead zone where you lose loop completion without gaining depth.

**Loop seam.** Last frame should visually match the first frame so the replay is invisible. Replays count as watch time.

**Captions always burned in.** Most viewing is muted. CapCut auto-captions, then manually fix the tool names — it will mangle every one of them.

**No trending audio for P2/P4.** Voice-led explainers lose to their own music. Use trending audio only on P1/P3/P5 where the visual carries it.

**Text on screen must be readable at thumbnail size.** Minimum ~90px equivalent at 1080x1920. If you have to squint, it's too small.

## 5. Posting cadence (48h sprint)

| Block | Time (IST) | Posts |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | 08:00–09:00 | 1 Reel |
| Midday | 13:00–14:00 | 1 Reel |
| Evening | 19:00–20:00 | 1 Reel (best slot) |
| Night | 22:00–23:00 | 1 Reel |

4 Reels/day = 8 over the sprint, plus 3–5 Stories/day. Posting more than ~5/day from a brand-new account looks automated and risks a review.

**Every post, within 20 minutes:** reply to every single comment. Comment replies in the first hour are a strong early-velocity signal and cost nothing.

## 6. Tactics that are legitimate and actually work

1. **Reply-bait CTA in the caption** — a real question, not "comment YES." e.g. "which model would you have trusted here?"
2. **Answer in the comments** — pin a comment that adds the one detail you left out of the video. Drives people back into the comment section.
3. **Story→Reel funnel** — post the Reel, then a Story pointing at it with a poll sticker. Polls have the highest Story interaction rate.
4. **Carousel for saves** — one carousel every other day. Carousels index for saves, which Reels don't do as well.
5. **Reply to comments with a video** — IG surfaces these natively and they get their own reach.
6. **Collabs** — the Collab feature puts one post on two accounts' grids and pools the reach. Find 3–5 accounts at a similar size and propose a genuine swap. This is the highest-leverage free tactic available and the one most people skip.
7. **Cross-post to TikTok/YouTube Shorts** — same asset, three platforms, zero extra production cost. Remove the IG watermark first (export from CapCut, not from IG).
8. **Keyword the first line of the caption** — IG search indexes caption text now. Lead with what the video is about in plain words.

## 7. Tactics that are off the table

Buying followers, follow/unfollow bots, engagement pods, mass-DM automation, comment bots, reposting others' content, and near-duplicate spam. All violate Instagram's Terms of Use. In 2026 they are also actively detected and the punishment is permanent reach suppression on the account — which would end the sprint, not accelerate it.

## 8. Production pipeline (all free)

```
idea  →  script (Claude/Gemini)
      →  screen record the actual workflow (macOS Cmd+Shift+5, free)
      →  voiceover (ElevenLabs free tier, 10k chars/mo)
      →  assemble + auto-caption (CapCut free)
      →  export 1080x1920, 30fps, no watermark
      →  post native in IG app/web (never a third-party poster)
```

Fallback if ElevenLabs quota runs out: macOS `say` command piped through ffmpeg, or Edge-TTS. Both free and unlimited.

## 9. Measurement

Check at +12h, +24h, +36h, +48h. Log in METRICS.md.

Per Reel, record: views, **watch-time %**, **sends**, saves, follows-from-this-post.

The only number that matters for iteration is **sends per 1000 views**. Rank every Reel by it. Whatever format is winning at +24h, that format takes 3 of the 4 slots on day 2. Kill whatever is bottom-quartile.

Vanity metrics to ignore: likes, total follower count in the first 24h.
