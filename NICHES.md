# Niche Shortlist — pick ONE

Scoring: /10. **Speed** = raw follower velocity. **Ship** = how fast I can produce it alone with free tools. **Send** = how likely a viewer DMs it to a friend (the #1 growth signal in 2026). **Ceiling** = how big this can get before it caps out.

---

## A. AI Tools & Workflows — "the thing you didn't know AI could do"
30–45s screen-recorded demos of a genuinely useful AI workflow. One tool, one outcome, per Reel.
- Speed 9 · Ship 10 · Send 10 · Ceiling 9
- **Why it wins:** fastest-growing IG niche in 2026, and it's the one where *you have an unfair advantage* — you have Gemini Pro + Claude Pro + ChatGPT and an AI agent building the content. Extremely high send-rate ("bro look at this"). Zero footage needed, zero face.
- **Risk:** crowded. Requires the demo to be actually surprising, not "here are 5 AI tools."

## B. Psychology / Dark Human Behaviour — "why people do that"
15–25s text-on-screen with a cinematic B-roll. One counterintuitive psychological mechanism per Reel.
- Speed 10 · Ship 9 · Send 10 · Ceiling 8
- **Why it wins:** documented as one of the two fastest follower-growth niches. Universal appeal, no audience gating. Viewers tag friends constantly ("this is literally you"). Saves + sends are enormous.
- **Risk:** saturated with low-effort accounts; needs a distinct visual signature to not blend in.

## C. Unsettling Science / Cosmic Scale — "space and physics facts that ruin your day"
30–50s slow-zoom visuals + voiceover. Awe or existential dread, every time.
- Speed 9 · Ship 8 · Send 9 · Ceiling 9
- **Why it wins:** highest completion rate of any faceless format — people watch to the end because the payoff is at the end. Gigantic global audience, no language/culture gating. Evergreen; content doesn't expire.
- **Risk:** needs good visuals to feel premium; more production time per Reel.

## D. Money Mechanics for Indian 20-somethings
15–30s, one specific money mechanic per Reel (how credit scores actually work, what an index fund really is, tax on freelance income).
- Speed 8 · Ship 9 · Send 8 · Ceiling 10
- **Why it wins:** finance is the other fastest-growth niche, and India-specific finance is far less saturated than US finance. Highest long-term monetization ceiling by a wide margin. You're in the target demographic, which makes the voice authentic.
- **Risk:** slower ceiling per-video than psychology/science; must avoid giving specific investment advice.

## E. Coding / Build-in-Public — "I built X in Y minutes"
30–50s timelapse of an actual thing being built, with the result shown first.
- Speed 6 · Ship 10 · Send 7 · Ceiling 8
- **Why it wins:** you genuinely do this, so it's real and defensible. Easiest possible production — it's a screen recording of your actual work. Strong, high-value audience.
- **Risk:** narrow audience → slowest raw follower growth of the list. Good for a career, bad for a 48h sprint.

## F. Historical "What Actually Happened" — one wild true story per Reel
30–50s narrative, archival imagery, strong cold open.
- Speed 8 · Ship 7 · Send 9 · Ceiling 8
- **Why it wins:** story format = best-in-class watch time. Extremely shareable. Evergreen library value.
- **Risk:** slowest to produce well (research + fact-check per video). Weakest fit for a 48h volume sprint.

## G. Talking Objects / POV Absurdism
30–50s, an inanimate object narrates its situation. AI-generated visuals + TTS.
- Speed 9 · Ship 8 · Send 9 · Ceiling 7
- **Why it wins:** the single best-performing faceless format on watch-time in 2026 per current data. Novel enough that people finish it. Purely entertainment → zero audience gating.
- **Risk:** entertainment audiences follow slowly and monetize poorly. Format may be near its peak already.

## H. "Things Nobody Tells You About India"
15–30s. Weirdly specific, verifiable facts about Indian systems, cities, laws, food, infrastructure.
- Speed 8 · Ship 9 · Send 10 · Ceiling 8
- **Why it wins:** IG's single largest user base is India — massive addressable audience, far less competition than the English-global niches. Comment-section debate is ferocious, which the algorithm loves. Enormously send-able within families and friend groups.
- **Risk:** must stay scrupulously factual and stay off religion/politics, or the account gets reported into oblivion.

---

## My recommendation

**A (AI Tools) as primary, B (Psychology) as the volume filler.**

Reasoning: A is where you have a real, unfair advantage — three paid AI subscriptions and an agent producing content — and it has the highest send-rate of anything on this list, which is the #1 reach signal. But A is limited by how many genuinely surprising demos exist, so B carries the posting volume: it's the cheapest possible content to produce at high quality and has the fastest raw follower velocity. Running two pillars also protects against the near-duplicate penalty.

If you want one single niche only: **B (Psychology)** for pure follower speed, **D (Indian money)** if you care more about what the account is worth in six months than what it is worth on Sunday.
