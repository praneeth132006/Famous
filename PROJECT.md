## 2. Reality check on the target (IMPORTANT — do not bury this)

10k followers in 48 hours from a zero-follower account is **not realistically achievable** through legitimate means. Documented cases of that speed are almost always one of:
- an existing audience migrating from another platform (YouTube/TikTok),
- a paid ad budget,
- a single freak viral hit (lottery odds, not a plan),
- bought/bot followers — which Instagram purges, and which tank reach permanently on the surviving account.

**What I will not do:** buy followers, use follow/unfollow bots, engagement pods, mass-DM automation, scraped-account spam, or repost other people's content as my own. These violate Instagram's Terms of Use, and in 2026 the algorithm explicitly de-ranks/excludes accounts doing them (10+ reposts in 30 days = removed from recommendations entirely). Doing this would burn the account inside the same 48 hours.

**What is actually achievable in 48h with hard execution:** a properly-built account, 12–20 high-quality Reels shipped, and a realistic range of **300–3,000 followers**, with a genuine (not guaranteed) shot at one Reel breaking 500k+ views, which is where the big numbers come from. The 48h sprint's real job is to build a *repeatable viral engine* — 10k then becomes a 2–4 week outcome instead of a 48h fantasy.

I'm going to run the full sprint at maximum intensity either way. Just recording honestly what the number will likely be.

## 3. Algorithm research findings (Sept 2026)

Sources: SocialPilot, Buffer, Hootsuite, Mosseri public statements.

Ranked signals for Reels reach in 2026:
1. **Watch time / completion rate** — #1 factor.
2. **Sends per reach** (DM shares) — Mosseri has called this the strongest single growth signal.
3. **Likes per reach.**
4. **Skip rate in first 3 seconds** — weak hook = distribution stops immediately.

Consequences for strategy:
- Follower count does NOT gate reach. Reels go primarily to non-followers. A day-one account can hit large numbers. This is the whole reason 48h is even worth attempting.
- **Optimal length: 7–15s for loopability, or 30–50s for depth.** Avoid the dead zone of 16–25s.
- **Originality premium:** original content gets 40–60% more distribution than reposts. Near-duplicate posting = 24h–30d penalty.
- **"Send-ability" is the design goal.** Every piece of content must answer: *why would someone DM this to a specific friend?* That is the growth lever, not likes.
- Narrow niche > broad niche. Tighter topic = better algorithmic matching = faster growth.
